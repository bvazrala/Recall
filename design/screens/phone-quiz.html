Which enzyme catalyzes the committed step of glycolysis?

  1. AHexokinase
  2. BPhosphofructokinase-1
  3. CPyruvate kinase
  4. DAldolaseyour answer

Not quite.

PFK-1 turns fructose-6-phosphate into fructose-1,6-bisphosphate. After that step the molecule can only continue down glycolysis, so it is the main control point.

Aldolase works one step later, splitting fructose-1,6-bisphosphate in two.
